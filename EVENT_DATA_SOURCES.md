- **Kodaikanal Observatory – Gundar Zero Point (Dindigul):** https://www.trektamilnadu.com/trail/solar-observatory-gundar-zero-point

- **Kumbakarai – Vellagavi – Kodaikanal (Camp Stay) (Dindigul):** https://www.trektamilnadu.com/trail/kumbakarai-vellagavi-kodaikanal-1-1
- **Kurangani – Sambalaru (Theni):** https://trektamilnadu.com/public/trail/kurangani-sambalaru

The public trail pages publish distance, difficulty, duration, listed package fee and inclusions. They do not publish a fixed future departure date in the catalogue; customers must select a live date on the portal. For camp-stay trips, confirm the actual overnight schedule and itinerary before paying.

## Scope

The app's Events & Adventures feed currently covers **Madurai and nearby Dindigul / Theni routes only**. Out-of-region event cards (including Erode, Coimbatore, Chennai and Tiruppur) were removed. Demo tournament fixtures remain visibly labelled as sample data and are not presented as verified public tournaments.
