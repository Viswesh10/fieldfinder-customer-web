# Madurai venue photo updates

This Customer experiment includes twelve new local image assets for the Madurai sports listings.

## Supplied venue photos

The following photographs were supplied by the project owner and mapped to their matching venue records:

- The Grid – Madurai
- NH Pickle Ball
- Pickle Arena
- SS Pickle Spot
- DUSA Pickleball – Kalavasal
- Snow White Pickleball Court
- SP Warzone
- Soldiers Boxing Academy & Baskar’s Sports Centre
- Valimai Sports Academy
- Phoenix Cros Fit

These records are tagged `venue-specific` and shown as `VENUE PHOTO`.

## Generated illustrative images

Two images were created to fill missing visual assets for:

- AGR Gaming Zone – Teppakulam
- Prakash Martial Arts & Fitness Academy

These are explicitly tagged `generated-illustration` and the UI labels them `ILLUSTRATIVE IMAGE`; they are not verified photos of those actual premises. Replace them with operator-supplied photos when available.
